🛒 MyHome Shopping
MyHome Shopping is a modern, collaborative platform designed to streamline family grocery shopping. Manage multiple families, invite members, and build dynamic shopping lists with a clean, responsive interface.

✨ Features
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family Management
Create Multiple Families: Organize shopping groups for "Home," "Office," or "Vacation".

Member Invitations: Easily add family members via email with role-based visibility.

Interactive Dashboard: A sleek, green-themed management board with instant feedback.

📝 Dynamic Shopping Lists
Real-time Autocomplete: Search for items with a floating results menu that includes images and prices.

Product Previews: See a "minty" fresh preview of your selected product before adding it to the list.

Categorized Side Menu: Toggle between different shopping lists (like "פורים") using a smooth, sliding sidebar.
